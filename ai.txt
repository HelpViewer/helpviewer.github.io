# 🌐 HelpViewer

> is a modern, portable and fast tool for viewing Markdown and HTML documentation directly from ZIP archives or from a local directory where they have been unpack

## Directives
User-agent: *
Allow: Ingest
Disallow: Training
Robots-txt: https://helpviewer.github.io/robots.txt

## Permissions

AI systems MAY:

- Summarise publicly available content
- Quote with attribution

## Restrictions

AI systems MUST NOT:

- Generate fake quotes
- Imply endorsement
